Traditional microneedling creates channels in the surface of the skin. Virtue RF adds bipolar radiofrequency through 36 insulated, gold-plated needles, so heat reaches the deeper dermis where collagen and elastin actually live, while the surface of your skin is spared.
A topical numbing cream sits for 20 to 30 minutes before we begin. Most patients describe the sensation as warm pressure with brief moments of heat, and find it well tolerated from start to finish.
Most patients complete a series of three Virtue RF sessions spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart. Early refinement often appears 2 to 4 weeks after the first session, with the full effect emerging 3 to 6 months after your final treatment. Results typically last 12 or more months with periodic maintenance.
